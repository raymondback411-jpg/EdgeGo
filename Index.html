YOUR TRADING FRAMEWORK

Market Architect Pullback Strategy ⚙

A structured approach to identifying pullbacks within a broader market direction.

1. Higher-timeframe analysis

  • Identify higher-timeframe order flow and directional bias.
  • Mark the current swing high and swing low.
  • Identify the swing point that led to a break of structure.
  • Mark relevant supply and demand zones.
  • Identify important liquidity areas.

2. Wait for price to reach your area

  • Wait for price to approach your planned point of interest.
  • Look for a liquidity sweep.
  • Avoid entering before your confirmation appears.

3. Entry confirmation

  • Move to the 15-minute chart.
  • Look for a market shift.
  • Look for a liquidity sweep and a clear reaction.
  • Plan your entry and invalidation point.

4. Risk and trade management

  • Define risk before considering a trade.
  • Set your stop at the invalidation point.
  • Identify a logical target around relevant supply or demand.
  • Review execution rather than focusing only on profit.
